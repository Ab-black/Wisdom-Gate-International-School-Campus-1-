About Us

Where Every Child's Potential Matters

At Wisdom Gate International School, education is about more than the classroom. It is about nurturing curiosity, building confidence, encouraging positive values, and preparing learners for the opportunities ahead.

Located in Rumuokoro, Port Harcourt, Campus 1 serves primary and secondary learners and welcomes parents as partners in their children's education.
